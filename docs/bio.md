# Joanna Wylon - Your Kraków Guide

## About Joanna

Joanna Wylon is a licensed professional tour guide based in Kraków, Poland, with well over 10 years of experience in the travel and tourism industry. As the founder of **Polish Adventures Joanna Wylon** (established 2015), she specializes in creating authentic, immersive experiences that showcase the best of Kraków and Poland through the lens of culinary traditions, cultural heritage, and outdoor adventures.

## Professional Background

### Tour Guiding Excellence

A tour guide by profession and a traveler by passion, Joanna brings a unique blend of local expertise and global perspective to every tour. Her extensive experience includes:

- **Tour guiding and management** - Leading both private and group tours throughout Kraków and Poland
- **Trip planning and coordination** - Crafting customized itineraries tailored to individual interests
- **Business travel management** - Organizing corporate events and business trips
- **Event organization** - Specializing in culinary experiences and outdoor activities

### Credentials & Memberships

- **Licensed tour guide** for Cracow (officially registered with FSP Kraków)
- **Beskidy Mountain Guide** at SKPG Kraków (Studenckie Koło Przewodników Górskich w Krakowie) since 2016
- **Member of PTTK** (Polish Tourist and Sightseeing Society) - Oddział Akademicki PTTK w Krakowie
- **Educational background:** Cracow School of Art and Fashion Design

### Leadership & Professional Service

Joanna has demonstrated exceptional commitment to the professional mountain guide community through extensive service in leadership roles at SKPG Kraków, Poland's oldest academic guide organization (founded 1955):

- **International Cooperation Manager** (2011-2017) - Fostering connections between Polish guides and international mountain guide associations
- **Secretary** (2013-2015) - Managing organizational communications and documentation
- **Board Member** (2013-2017) - Contributing to strategic decisions and organizational development
- **Revision Committee Member** (2017-2019) - Ensuring organizational transparency and compliance

SKPG Kraków, part of the Academic Section of PTTK, is a prestigious organization that has trained over 1,160 mountain guides throughout its 70+ year history. The organization promotes active and sustainable tourism as well as outdoor education, and consists of professional mountain guides with state qualifications for the Beskidy and Tatra Mountain regions.

## Specializations

### Culinary Tours

With a deep passion for Polish gastronomy and food culture, Joanna specializes in culinary experiences that go beyond typical tourist restaurants. Her food tours reveal the authentic tastes of Kraków, from traditional pierogi workshops to modern Polish cuisine, local markets, and hidden culinary gems known only to locals.

Guests consistently praise her ability to weave together Kraków's rich history with its culinary traditions, sharing fascinating facts about the city while introducing them to authentic Polish flavors. Her tours are known for being informative, delicious, and suitable for various dietary preferences including vegetarian options.

### Outdoor & Nature Adventures

Mountains are the most important thing in Joanna's life, giving her what she describes as "the feeling of freedom." This deep passion infuses her outdoor tour offerings, where she leads nature-focused experiences that combine cultural exploration with outdoor activities, including:
- Hiking and trekking in the Tatra Mountains and Beskidy ranges
- Camping expeditions
- Nature walks combining history and natural beauty
- Outdoor sports and adventure activities

As a certified Beskidy Mountain Guide with state qualifications, she brings both professional expertise and genuine enthusiasm to every mountain adventure.

## Global Perspective

Joanna's extensive travel experience sets her apart from typical local guides. She has visited **over 50 countries across 6 continents**, bringing a global perspective that helps her understand what international visitors seek while appreciating what makes Poland unique. This worldview allows her to bridge cultures and create meaningful connections between visitors and the places they explore.

## Beyond Guiding: NGO Work & Community

For more than 14 years, Joanna has been a dedicated NGO worker, primarily as a volunteer. Her journey with international cooperation began in 2010 when she participated in a youth exchange program in the Czech Republic through **IYNF (International Young Naturefriends)**. This experience proved transformative, leading her to complete a **European Voluntary Service (EVS)** assignment at the IYNF office in Prague, where she worked until 2012.

Her deep commitment to the organization's mission led to her election as **Vice-President of IYNF in 2014**, a role through which she continued fostering international youth cooperation and promoting sustainable tourism values.

Her broader community involvement includes:
- Active engagement in youth organizations and cultural exchange programs
- Promoting sustainable tourism and environmental awareness
- Building bridges between international communities through tourism and outdoor education

This volunteer work reflects her deeper mission: not just showing visitors around, but fostering genuine cultural understanding and connection between people from different backgrounds.
