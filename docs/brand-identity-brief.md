# KrakowBites - Brand Identity Brief

**Date:** 2026-01-01
**Project:** Solo guide booking platform for food & heritage tours in Kraków
**Target:** Design guide for logo, color palette, typography, and visual system

---

## Brand Essence

### Name Rationale
**KrakowBites**
- "Bites" emphasizes food tours (primary differentiator)
- Also works metaphorically for heritage tours (bite-sized history stories)
- Memorable, pronounceable internationally, easy to spell
- Playful but not childish, approachable but professional

### Brand Promise
"Taste Kraków's forgotten stories with a local guide"

### Positioning Statement
KrakowBites offers narrative-driven food and heritage tours led by a passionate solo guide who reveals the untold stories of Polish and Jewish Kraków through immersive culinary and historical experiences - not generic walking tours, but curated journeys into the city's soul.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Cultural Travelers (25-55 years old)
- **Values:** Authenticity, learning, local experiences over tourist traps
- **Behavior:** Research extensively before trips, read reviews, seek unique experiences
- **Pain points:** Generic group tours feel impersonal, want deeper cultural understanding
- **Desires:** Personal connection to places through food and stories

### Secondary: History Enthusiasts
- **Interests:** WWII history, Jewish heritage, pre-war European culture
- **Motivations:** Respect and remembrance, educational travel
- **Expectations:** Knowledgeable guide, sensitivity, historical accuracy

### Psychographics
- Educated, curious, open-minded
- Instagram users but not influencers (share experiences authentically)
- Willing to pay premium for quality over discount tours
- Value small groups, personalized attention

---

## Brand Personality

### Voice Attributes
- **Warm** but not overly familiar - friendly guide, not best friend
- **Knowledgeable** but not academic - storyteller, not lecturer
- **Respectful** especially with heritage content - thoughtful, never exploitative
- **Authentic** - real stories, real places, no tourist-trap fluff
- **Inviting** - "join me" not "buy from me"

### Tone Examples

**Good:**
> "Before World War II, Kazimierz's bakeries filled the air with challah on Friday afternoons. Grandmothers passed down gefilte fish recipes that varied from family to family, street to street. These flavors disappeared in 1939. But through the work of historians and devoted chefs, we can taste them again."

**Bad (too corporate):**
> "Experience authentic Jewish cuisine on our highly-rated Kazimierz food tour. Book now for best availability!"

**Bad (too casual):**
> "OMG the pierogi here are INSANE! You're gonna love this spot! 🔥"

---

## Visual Direction

### Brand Archetypes
**Primary:** The Sage (knowledgeable guide, wisdom, authenticity)
**Secondary:** The Explorer (discovery, adventure, curiosity)

### Mood Board Keywords
- **Heritage:** Warm sepia tones, aged brick, cobblestone streets, vintage typography
- **Food:** Golden brown bread crusts, steaming soups, colorful markets, rustic wooden tables
- **Modern touch:** Clean layouts, readable sans-serif, white space, elegant photography

### Avoid
- ❌ Tourist-trap red/white Polish flag clichés
- ❌ Communist-era grey brutalism aesthetics
- ❌ Generic food blog pastels (too soft)
- ❌ Dark/heavy Holocaust imagery (respectful but not somber brand)
- ❌ Overly ornate Eastern European folk patterns (dated)

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

**Golden Wheat** `#D4A574`
- Evokes: Fresh bread, challah crust, warmth, comfort
- Usage: Primary CTA buttons, headers, accents
- Associations: Traditional baking, harvest, nourishment

**Deep Paprika** `#8B3A3A`
- Evokes: Polish cuisine spice, brick buildings of Kazimierz, warmth
- Usage: Secondary accents, hover states, highlights
- Associations: Passion, heritage, depth

**Cream Linen** `#F5F1E8`
- Evokes: Parchment, aged documents, tablecloths, simplicity
- Usage: Backgrounds, cards, subtle sections
- Associations: Cleanliness, tradition, elegance

### Secondary Colors

**Sage Green** `#7A8B70`
- Evokes: Fresh herbs (dill, parsley in Polish cooking), market vegetables
- Usage: Success states, dietary restriction icons
- Associations: Fresh ingredients, health, nature

**Charcoal** `#3A3A3A`
- Evokes: Text, readability, sophistication
- Usage: Body text, headings
- Associations: Professionalism, clarity

**Soft White** `#FFFFFF`
- Usage: Backgrounds, breathing room
- Associations: Cleanliness, modernity

### Accent Colors (Minimal Use)

**Amber Alert** `#E6A047`
- Usage: "Spots filling up fast" urgency indicators
- Associations: Caution (positive), limited availability

**Steel Blue** `#4A6B7C`
- Usage: Links, informational elements
- Associations: Trust, reliability

### Color Usage Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use Golden Wheat for primary CTAs ("Book Now", "View Details")
- Use Deep Paprika sparingly for emphasis (tour category badges, icons)
- Keep 70% Cream/White for breathing room, 20% Golden/Paprika accents, 10% other
- Maintain high contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum)

**Don't:**
- Mix too many colors on one screen (3-4 max per view)
- Use pure black (#000000) - use Charcoal instead for softer feel
- Overuse red tones (can feel aggressive or Communist-era)

## Typography

### Heading Font: **Playfair Display** (Serif)
- **Why:** Elegant, readable, evokes historical documents, sophisticated
- **Usage:** H1, H2, tour names, quotes, storytelling sections
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Bold (700)
- **Pairs well with:** Modern sans-serif for contrast

**Example:**
```
H1: 48px, Bold - Homepage hero headline
H2: 36px, Bold - Section titles
H3: 28px, Regular - Tour names
```

### Body Font: **Inter** (Sans-serif)
- **Why:** Highly readable, modern, neutral, excellent for UI
- **Usage:** Body text, navigation, buttons, forms, all functional text
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600)

**Example:**
```
Body: 16px, Regular - Paragraphs, descriptions
Button: 14px, Semibold - CTAs
Nav: 15px, Medium - Menu items
```

### Alternative (if Playfair feels too formal): **Merriweather** (Serif)
- Softer, more approachable than Playfair
- Still elegant but less ornate

### Font Pairing Example
```css
h1, h2, h3 {
  font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;
  color: #3A3A3A;
}

body, p, button, nav {
  font-family: 'Inter', sans-serif;
  color: #3A3A3A;
}

.tour-tagline {
  font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;
  font-style: italic;
  font-size: 18px;
  color: #8B3A3A;
}
```

## Logo Concepts

### Direction 1: Typography-First (Recommended)

**Concept:**
- "KrakowBites" in custom lettering
- "Krakow" in Playfair Display serif (elegant, historical)
- "Bites" in Inter sans-serif (modern, approachable)
- Subtle accent: Small fork icon integrated into the 'i' or 't'
- Tagline beneath: "Food & Heritage Tours" (Inter, smaller)

**Why:**
- Readable, scalable, professional
- Combines heritage (serif) with modern (sans-serif)
- Minimal icon = timeless design
- Works in single color for versatility

**Sketch:**
```
Krakow BITES
━━━━━━━━━━━━
Food & Heritage Tours
   (fork icon replacing 'i' dot)
```

---

### Direction 2: Iconic Emblem

**Concept:**
- Circular badge with:
  - Outer ring: "KrakowBites" text curved
  - Center icon: Pierogi outline OR Star of David + fork crossed
  - Est. 2026 (or guide's first year with Walkative)
- Vintage stamp aesthetic

**Why:**
- Memorable visual identity
- Badge works well for stickers, prints, social media profile
- Heritage feel (like traditional bakery or restaurant seals)

**Considerations:**
- Risk: Can feel dated if not executed cleanly
- Complexity: May not scale well to very small sizes (favicon)

---

### Direction 3: Minimalist Mark

**Concept:**
- Simple geometric icon: Bread loaf + fork overlap
- "KrakowBites" wordmark (all sans-serif, clean)
- Single color or two-tone (Golden Wheat + Charcoal)

**Why:**
- Modern, scalable, works at any size
- Easy to animate for web
- Less personality but more versatile

**Considerations:**
- Risk: May feel too generic/startup-y
- Needs strong photography to add warmth

---

### Recommended Approach: **Direction 1 (Typography-First)**
- More sophisticated than minimalist mark
- More scalable than emblem
- Balances heritage and modernity
- Lets photography and content shine (logo doesn't compete)

## Photography Style Guide

### Food Photography
**Do:**
- Natural lighting, warm tones
- Close-ups showing texture (bread crust, steam, garnish)
- Styled but not overproduced (authentic, not magazine perfection)
- Include hands, plates, dining context (not just isolated food)
- Polish and Jewish dishes with equal visual weight

**Don't:**
- Sterile white backgrounds (feels lab-like)
- Overhead flat-lays (overdone Instagram trend)
- Over-saturated colors (unrealistic)
- Stock photos (obvious and generic)

**Reference Style:** Bon Appétit editorial photography (warm, inviting, real)

---

### Heritage/Location Photography
**Do:**
- Golden hour lighting when possible (warm, nostalgic)
- Capture people (tourists, guide) engaged with location (not empty streets)
- Mix wide shots (context) with detail shots (architectural elements)
- Respectful framing for memorial sites (no selfies at Auschwitz)
- Black & white for historical gravitas (sparingly)

**Don't:**
- Gloomy, grey, depressing tones (even for serious sites)
- Tourist-trap angles (same shot everyone has)
- Overly HDR-processed (looks fake)

**Reference Style:** National Geographic travel photography (storytelling, authentic, respectful)

---

### Guide Photography (About Page)
**Do:**
- Candid shots in Kraków settings (market, café, street)
- Warm smile, welcoming posture, eye contact with camera
- Natural colors, not overly retouched
- Show personality (maybe holding pierogi, laughing with group)

**Don't:**
- Corporate headshot on plain background (too formal)
- Overly posed influencer aesthetic (inauthentic)
- Dark, moody lighting (uninviting)

## Accessibility Requirements

### Color Contrast
- All text must meet WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 for normal text)
- Golden Wheat (#D4A574) on White: 3.1:1 ❌ (use for large text only or darken)
- Charcoal (#3A3A3A) on White: 10.7:1 ✅ (excellent)
- Deep Paprika (#8B3A3A) on Cream Linen: 5.2:1 ✅ (good)

**Fix for Golden Wheat text:**
- Darken to #B8854F for small text on white backgrounds
- Or use only on buttons (not body text)

### Readability
- Minimum font size: 16px for body text
- Line height: 1.6-1.8 for paragraphs
- Max line width: 70 characters (optimal readability)

### Interactive Elements
- All buttons minimum 44x44px touch target
- Focus states clearly visible (outline or ring)
- Keyboard navigation supported
